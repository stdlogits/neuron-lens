You are describing attention heads of Gemma 4 12B, a base model (48 layers, 16 query heads each), from evidence cards. Most layers attend only within the last 1,024 tokens, and their 16 query heads share 8 key-value heads; every sixth layer (5, 11, …, 47) is global, attending over the whole text, with one key-value head whose keys are also its values. The card says which kind of layer a head is in. Position 0, the sink, is the `<bos>` token that opens every text.
